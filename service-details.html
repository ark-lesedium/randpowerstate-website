Digital Platforms & Web Systems

This covers everything from your public-facing website to the tools your staff log into every day. We design them to still work well two or three years from now, not just on launch day — so you're not paying to rebuild from scratch every time your business grows.

  • Business and franchise websites customers can find, trust, and book or buy from
  • Staff portals and dashboards for things like stock, bookings, or sales — without needing a spreadsheet
  • A secure, organised place to store contracts, records, and other documents

IT Systems Integration

If your customer database, accounting package, and booking calendar all run separately, someone on your team is copying information between them by hand — and mistakes creep in. We connect those tools so an update in one place shows up everywhere it needs to.

  • Linking your CRM (customer records) and ERP (accounting/operations) so they share data automatically
  • Wiring up analytics and reporting tools so your numbers update themselves
  • Making sure the same customer or sale looks identical everywhere it appears

Workflow Automation & Process Digitisation

Paper forms, email approval chains, and manually-built reports all take time and are easy to lose track of. We turn those steps into a digital process that runs itself — so approvals happen faster and nothing falls through the cracks.

  • Approval and sign-off steps that route themselves to the right person automatically
  • Reports that generate on a schedule instead of being pulled together by hand
  • Turning paper-based forms and checklists into simple digital ones

AI-Enabled Business Intelligence

Your systems are already collecting sales, stock, and customer data — most of it just sits unused. We set up AI tools that read that data and turn it into plain-language answers, like which products are slow-moving or which days need more staff, so decisions don't rely on guesswork.

  • Reports that flag trends before they become problems, not just after
  • Plain-language summaries and answers instead of raw spreadsheets
  • Automation that's there because it saves real time, not for novelty

Cybersecurity & Infrastructure Support

Weak passwords, outdated software, and unprotected customer data are how small businesses get hurt. We lock those gaps down, keep systems patched and backed up, and stay on call so a hack, outage, or lost device doesn't bring your business to a stop.

  • Locked-down logins and controls over who can access what
  • Ongoing monitoring so outages get caught and fixed quickly, not discovered by a customer
  • Security considered at every step of a build, not added as an afterthought
