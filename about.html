Our Mission

We build the website, and the software behind it, so your business runs smoother — not a site that just sits there looking nice.

We Ask "What Does This Need to Do?" Before "What Should It Look Like?"

Most agencies sell you a website and move on. We stick around to build and maintain the software working behind it — the part that actually saves your team time.

RANDPOWERSTATE (PTY) LTD is based in Kimberley, Northern Cape, and builds websites, business software, and IT systems for retail stores, restaurants and cafés, clinics, fuel stations, and franchise operators. These are businesses that need more than a brochure site — they need a website and back-end systems that can handle real daily traffic, real customer data, and real staff using them every day.

Before we design anything, we ask what the system needs to actually do for your business, day to day — who'll use it, what it needs to connect to, and what happens if it goes down. Getting that right comes first. How it looks comes after.

The Difference Structure Makes

What Usually Happens vs. What We Do Instead

The Common Approach

  • The website goes live, then gets forgotten until something breaks.
  • Every new tool means someone re-typing the same information by hand.
  • Security becomes a priority only after something has already gone wrong.
  • One extra request means finding and briefing a whole new freelancer.

How We Work

  • We stay on after launch to monitor, maintain, and adjust the system.
  • Your tools are connected, so information updates itself.
  • Security is built in from the first line of code, not added later.
  • One team handles the website, the systems, and the support behind it.
